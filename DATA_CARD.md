# FraudShield data card

Generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py` (code: `fraudshield/data/`). Every number below comes from a real run
(`data/processed/build_manifest.json`).

## Source and license
- Olist Brazilian E-Commerce public dataset (Kaggle `olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce`, files in `data/raw/`), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
  non-commercial, share-alike. Fine-tuned models trained on it must not be pushed to a public commercial repo.
- 100,010 bookings (one per order_id x seller_id pair), 3,095 seller accounts, 2016-09 to 2018-09. Asserted in
  `tests/data/test_olist.py::test_real_olist_counts`.

## What is real, synthetic, injected

| Column | Kind | How |

| account_id, sender_id | real | seller_id (sender = account for every real row) |
| booked_at | real | order_approved_at, fallback order_purchase_timestamp (14 rows) |
| first_scan_at | real | order_delivered_carrier_date (missing for about 1% of rows) |
| delivered_at, estimated_at, purchase_at, order_status | real | orders table |
| origin_zip5/zip3/uf | real | seller zip prefix and state (replaced on synthetic new-warehouse rows, flagged `origin_synthetic`) |
| consignee_id, dest_zip5/zip3/uf | real | customer_unique_id, customer zip prefix and state |
| distance_km | derived | haversine between zip5-prefix centroids of the real geolocation table (fallback zip3, then state) |
| weight_kg | real | sum of item catalog weights (catalog values, not measured) |
| length_cm, width_cm, height_cm | derived | max item length, max item width, sum of item heights (items stacked in one box) |

| declared_value | real | sum of item price |

| service | derived [ASSUMPTION] | express if the promised window (estimated - purchase) is in the fastest 20% of its distance band |
| channel, device_id, login_device_age_days, payment_method, payer_instrument_id, owner_contact_age_days | SYNTHETIC | billing layer, one fixed seed (20261002) for all real accounts |

| is_injected, scenario_id, campaign_id, typology, camouflage_level, mimic, params_json, seed, phase, true_weight_kg | INJECTED metadata | set on every injected row; real rows: is_injected False, typology "none", seed -1 |
| is_fraud, label_misuse, label_foreign_senders, label_payoff_max, label_drop_consignee, risk_level, cost_ratio | labels | from injection truth; real rows are assumed legit |
| split | derived | time split (below) |

Olist has no device, login, payer or contact data. None of those columns are real.

## Synthetic billing layer (`fraudshield/data/billing_layer.py`, seed 20261002)
All parameters are assumptions:
- account_created_at: first booking minus U(30, 365) days if the first booking is before 2017-03, else U(1, 30).
- payment_method: account_billing 55%, ach 25%, card 20%; one payer instrument per account.
- 1 to 3 logins (Poisson(1)+1, capped), each a device with a link date; channel api 50% for accounts with >= 50 bookings, else web
  (5% api, 5% counter). The first login is used 3x as often as the others.
- Owner contact on file from account creation; 10% of accounts have no verified contact.
- Legit changes on accounts with >= 20 bookings (hard negatives), at a random date 30-80% through the account's life:
  new_warehouse 2% (half the later bookings ship from another real zip in the same state), new_login 3% (new device used
  for 60% of later bookings), new_instrument 5%, bundled_expansion 1% (all three within one week). Realized on the real
  data: new_instrument 32, new_login 24, new_warehouse 18, bundled_expansion 5 accounts. Pre-announced through the
  verified change flow with p = 0.3 / 0.2 / 0.2 / 0.5, announced 1 to 7 days before the change.

## Injected typologies (`fraudshield/data/inject.py`)
Rule-based (no GAN). Master seed 20261002, injection seed k uses 20261002 + k; 10 seeds built (k = 0..9).
Campaigns per 1,000 real bookings in a split: T1 0.27, T4 0.22, T2 0.18, T3 0.09 (rings), T5 0.18, T6 0.18, T7 0.22,
HN_new_channel 0.18, HN_3pl 0.13. Camouflage level per campaign: 0 (50%), 1 (30%), 2 (20%); mimic knob on with p = 0.3
(always at level 2). Campaigns are truncated at their split's end, and victims are drawn from disjoint per-split account
pools (40% train, 20% cal, 40% test), so victims never repeat across splits within a seed.

Camouflage: level 1 restricts origins and destinations to the victim's own states, matches the victim's hours and uses no
new login; level 2 adds the victim's own contents and caps volume at the victim's p90 daily volume, and removes links to
other campaigns. Campaign sizes are much smaller than in the sources (DOJ cases involve thousands of labels) to keep
prevalence near 1%.

Snapping to real grids: parcels (weight, dims, category, value, item count) are real Olist parcels; zips are real zip
prefixes; freight is a real freight value drawn from the same weight x distance bin (12 x 12 quantile bins); scan lags
are real lags; time of day uses the real distribution (or the victim's own hours) and seconds-of-minute are drawn from
the real approval timestamps; ids use the same hex formats as Olist.

Confirmed-fraud feedback (simulated): every fraud campaign is "confirmed" 30 days after its last booking; its consignees
and third-party sender ids become known fraud entities from then on (feature `links_confirmed_fraud`, tier F).

Realized counts (all 10 seeds): 15,529 injected rows, 8,563 fraud rows. Test window: 300 fraud campaigns (T1 60, T2 40,
T3 20 rings, T4 50, T5 40, T6 40, T7 50) and 70 injected-legit campaigns. Prevalence of fraud bookings per seed: train
0.83-1.02%, cal 0.84-1.11%, test 1.17-1.41%.

## Splits (`fraudshield/data/split.py`)
warm-up < 2017-06-01 (history only); train 2017-06-01..2018-01-31; embargo; calibration 2018-02-15..2018-04-30;
embargo; test 2018-05-15..2018-08-31. Real bookings: warm-up 11,547, train 41,042, cal 17,920, test 22,435,
embargo 7,065, after 1. T3 and T5 appear only in test.

## Known limitations
- freight_value is buyer-paid marketplace freight, used as a proxy for carrier cost.
- No device, login, IP, payer or contact data exists in Olist; the whole tier F layer is synthetic. Tier R results use
  real columns only and are reported separately.
- Real third-party billing never occurs in Olist, so in tier F "sender differs from account" is rare among legit rows
  (only the injected 3PL hard negatives have it). T1 and T7 are therefore easy in tier F; tier R numbers are the fairer view.
- Weights and dims are catalog values, not measured; the service tier is a proxy; domestic only (no international drops).
- Real rows are assumed legit; Olist could contain undetected fraud (label noise).
- The RBA login dataset, LaDe and Amazon Last Mile are not used.
- Artifact check (scripts/sanity_checks.py): injected-legit vs real on columns with a real counterpart reaches grouped
  AUC 0.617 (target 0.6, placebo floor 0.531), so the injected hard negatives remain mildly distinguishable; non-semantic
  columns pass (0.535).
- Campaigns are short (prevalence target), so per-campaign evidence accumulates over fewer bookings than in real cases.

## Intended use
Prototype evaluation of booking-time fraud scoring on documented injected typologies. Claims are limited to "detects our
documented injections of these typologies on real shipper histories", not real-world fraud rates.
