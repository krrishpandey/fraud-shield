LightGBM and the app score: how well it learns and how accurate it is

The scoring model, measured on the test window (2018-05-15 to 2018-08-31). The "app score" is what the decision service uses: LightGBM plus a drop-address rule from published research (Hao et al., CCS 2015). Fraud is rare (about 1.3% of bookings), so the charts use PR-AUC, precision and recall rather than accuracy.

Why not plain accuracy? A model that calls every booking legitimate is already accurate and catches no fraud at all. PR-AUC asks the useful question: when the model ranks bookings by risk, how much of the fraud sits at the top? It runs from 0 to 1, and random guessing scores about .
