LightGBM: how well it learns and how accurate it is

FraudShield's backup scoring model, measured on the test window (2018-05-15 to 2018-08-31). Fraud is rare (about 1.3% of bookings), so the charts use PR-AUC, precision and recall rather than accuracy.

Why not plain accuracy? A model that calls every booking legitimate is already accurate and catches no fraud at all. PR-AUC asks the useful question: when the model ranks bookings by risk, how much of the fraud sits at the top? It runs from 0 to 1, and random guessing scores about .
